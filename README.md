# A477/A577: TRENDS IN EXTREME WEATHER

This course provides students with a scientific understanding of the impacts of climate change on extreme weather events. Throughout the semester, we will review statistical methods and peer-reviewed literature related to extreme weather and attribution.

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Apply foundational statistical methods to analyze extreme weather data and climate reanalysis fields.
- Explain Generalized Extreme Value (GEV) theory, the Central Limit Theorem, and their roles in Detection & Attribution (D&A) methodologies.
- Evaluate various D&A frameworks, identifying their underlying assumptions, statistical power, and limitations.
- Critically analyze and classify peer-reviewed literature on climate extremes according to methodological approach.
- Summarize the current state of scientific knowledge regarding anthropogenic climate impacts on extreme weather and articulate its broader societal and ecological implications.
